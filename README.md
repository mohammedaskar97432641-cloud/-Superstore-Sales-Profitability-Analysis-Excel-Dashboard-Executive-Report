📊 Superstore Sales & Profitability Analysis | Excel Dashboard & Executive Report
About The Project
A comprehensive data analytics project examining retail and office supplies performance for Superstore (generating $2.297M in total sales). The project aims to diagnose the root causes of financial losses across underperforming states and product categories, evaluate the impact of excessive discounting on net profitability, and deliver actionable strategic recommendations to expand profit margins.

🔑 Key Financial Indicators
Total Revenue: $2,297,201 ($2,297,200.86)

Net Profit: $286,397 ($286,397.02)

Total Losses: -$156,131 (-$156,131.29)

Profitable vs. Loss-Making Line Items: 8,058 profitable items vs. 1,871 loss-making items (and 65 break-even items)

Most Profitable State: California ($76,381.39 in profit from $457,687.63 in sales)

Highest Loss-Making State: Texas (-$25,729.36 in net loss)

💡 Key Business Insights & Recommendations
Discount Impact Analysis: Offering discounts above 20% was identified as the primary driver behind eroding margins in categories such as Furniture (Tables & Bookcases) and pushing Texas transactions into negative profitability.

Executive Recommendation: Restructure the discounting policy by capping discounts between 5% and 9% for margin-sensitive items, while focusing sales growth efforts on products within the $1 – $1,000 profit tier.

🛠️ Technical Implementation & Features
Data Modeling & Pivot Tables: Cleaned, structured, and modeled raw transactional data using dynamic Pivot Tables to calculate key financial metrics.

Interactive Dashboard: Designed a dynamic executive dashboard linking multiple charts to unified Slicers for seamless filtering by Year, Region, and Category.

Geographic Mapping: Built a state-level Filled Map visualizing profit distribution across the United States.

Security & Protection: Applied Very Hidden sheet protection to backend Pivot Table tabs and locked the workbook structure to safeguard formulas and dashboard integrity.
